By Kenney Vleugels (kenney.nl), **CC0 1.0**: commercial use OK, credit optional. Each pack's `License.txt` is copied alongside. Only the files we use are copied here, unmodified. Trims, fades and gain are applied at mix time by `tools/cast_sfx_mp4.py`.
